AGE RATING (App Store Connect > App Information > Age Ratings > Set Up Age Ratings)

Source of the question list: App Store Connect Help, "Age ratings values and definitions" and "Set an app age rating", both read 2026-10-11 (the 2026 questionnaire: in-app controls, capabilities, mature themes, medical or wellness, sexuality or nudity, violence, chance-based activities). Wording in the dialog may differ slightly; the meaning of each question is quoted from Apple's page.

THE ONE FACT THAT DECIDES EVERYTHING
IPTVMac contains no content. It plays what the user's own source delivers, from servers the user chooses. The app cannot see, filter or rate that content, and IPTV lists in the real world often contain channels for adults. So there are two honest layers: what the app itself contains (nothing) and what it can show (anything the user's source serves). Answer the questionnaire about the first, then decide the override for the second (see the end).

ANSWERS
In-app controls
  Parental Controls ................ No. The Settings "Hide categories" word filter says in the app that it is "a plain filter, not a parental lock: anyone can change it here". Declaring it as parental controls would be false.
  Age Assurance .................... No. The app never asks for or checks an age.
Capabilities
  Unrestricted Web Access .......... No. Apple's definition: "Users can navigate to any webpage within the app or freely browse the web. May include: embedded browser functionality or browser app." IPTVMac has no web view and no browser. It opens only the playlist and stream addresses the user typed (media, never web pages). The one link to a web page, Trailer, hands a YouTube address to the user's own browser outside the app.
                                     Honest caveat: a user can type any server address. If you read the question as "can the user reach arbitrary third-party media", the answer is yes. That reading is covered by the override below, not by ticking a box that describes something else.
  User-Generated Content ........... No. Apple's definition: "the broad distribution of content created by users as a component of the app's intended user experience". Users cannot create, upload, post or share anything with other users. (Favorites, history and backups stay on the Mac.)
  Social Media ..................... No.   Social Media disabled for users under 13: not applicable.
  Messaging and Chat ............... No.
  Advertising ...................... No. No ads of any kind.

CALCULATED RESULT: 4+ (all answers above).

AGE CATEGORIES AND OVERRIDE (same dialog, last step)
  Made for Kids ............ Not applicable. Do NOT select it (it commits the app to the Kids category rules, and 2.3.8 reserves "For Kids" wording for that category).
  Override to Higher Age Rating ... RECOMMENDED: 18+ (16+ is the least I would accept).
    Reasoning (my recommendation, the owner decides):
    - Apple's own page says to override "if ... you believe your app should be rated higher", and that the content descriptions then still reflect your answers. The listing keeps its true descriptors and only the rating goes up.
    - 16+ is the level Apple attaches to Unrestricted Web Access; 18+ covers frequent sexual content or nudity. A player that opens any source a user adds can display either. Real IPTV lists often carry adult categories.
    - The risk is asymmetric. Guideline 2.3.6: "If your app is mis-rated, customers might be surprised by what they get, or it could trigger an inquiry from government regulators." An under-rated app can be rejected or pulled; an over-rated one only loses some audience (children are not the audience of an IPTV player).
    - Regional note on the same Apple page: a 17+ (pre-26 scale) rating shows as 18+ in France; nothing to do for that.
  Age Suitability URL (optional) ..... https://goelir.github.io/IPTVMac/support/ (it says the app has no content of its own and that the user must use only sources they are authorized to access).

If the owner prefers the literal 4+: say so in the review notes (already done in review-notes.txt: "no content of its own") and expect a possible reviewer request to raise it; raising it later is a metadata change, not a new build.

METADATA RULE THAT STAYS 4+ (2.3.8): icons, screenshots and previews must be fit for a 4+ rating even if the app is rated higher. The screenshots in docs/screenshots use invented titles and gradient posters; the player shot is Sintel (animation). Nothing to change.
