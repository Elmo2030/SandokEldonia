APP PREVIEW VIDEO (optional) (App Store Connect > the version > Mac app previews)

SPEC (App Store Connect Help, "App preview specifications", read 2026-10-11)
- Mac: 1920 x 1080, landscape only. H.264 (.mp4/.m4v/.mov), 15 to 30 seconds, max 30 fps, max 500 MB, target 10 to 12 Mbps, stereo AAC 256 kbps at 44.1 or 48 kHz.
- Guideline 2.3.4: a preview may only be a video screen capture of the app itself; narration and text overlays are allowed. No other platforms, no marketing footage.
- Up to 3 previews per size and language. Processing up to 24 hours after upload. A preview is always shown before the screenshots.

THE EXISTING DEMO (docs/assets/demo.mp4)
- 1280 x 720, 79.5 s, H.264 30 fps, AAC stereo 48 kHz, 2.5 MB. It is too long (max 30 s) and too small (needs 1920 x 1080). It cannot be uploaded as is. Upscaling 720p to 1080p works technically but looks soft: record again at 1080p if you want a preview, or skip the preview (screenshots are enough to be approved).

Credit stays required in the App Store version: put a caption "Sintel (c) Blender Foundation, CC BY 3.0, durian.blender.org" on screen while the footage plays (text overlays are allowed), and keep the same credit in the description (done).

  ffmpeg -i docs/assets/demo.mp4 -ss 0 -t 28 -vf "scale=1920:1080:flags=lanczos,fps=30" \
    -c:v libx264 -b:v 10M -maxrate 12M -bufsize 20M -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac -b:a 256k -ar 48000 -ac 2 -movflags +faststart preview-1080p.mp4
  Check which 28 seconds show the best moments (search, player, Picture in Picture) with a player before cutting; use -ss to start later.
