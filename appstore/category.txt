CATEGORY (App Store Connect > App Information > Category)

Primary:   Video
Secondary: Utilities   (optional; leaving it empty is also fine)

Why Video
- IPTVMac is a media player. Players of this kind (IINA, VLC) live in the Video category on the Mac App Store (from memory; check the dropdown, the list of Mac categories is Apple's and may change).
- Guideline 2.3.5 asks for the most appropriate category and says Apple may change one that is "way off base". Video is what the app does; it carries no content of its own.

Why not Entertainment
- The Age Ratings page of App Store Connect Help (read 2026-10-11, "Republic of Korea regional ratings") says apps in the Games or Entertainment categories, primary or secondary, display an additional regional rating from the Korean GRAC, and that App Review may ask for a Rating Classification Number (RCN). A free player has no reason to take that on.
- Entertainment also invites reviewers to compare the app with content apps, and IPTVMac has no content.

Why Utilities as the secondary
- It describes what the app is for (a tool for the user's own sources) without the Korea rule above. Do not pick News, Sports or Music: the app has no editorial content.

Not recommended: Social Networking (the app has no accounts and no user content).

Re-check when submitting: if App Store Connect offers different names, keep the intent (a media player, no Entertainment).
