IPTVMac is a native Mac player for your own IPTV playlists. Add an M3U playlist link or an Xtream Codes login from a provider you are authorized to use, and browse your live channels, movies and series in a fast, clean app.

IPTVMac is only a player. It includes no channels, movies, series or subscriptions, and it needs a source of your own to show anything.

GET STARTED IN THREE STEPS
1. Choose M3U or Xtream Codes.
2. Enter a name and the playlist link, or the server address, username and password from your provider.
3. Click Test connection, then Save and sync. Your lists appear under Live, Movies and Series.

FIND THINGS FAST
• Instant search across all your channels, movies and series, in Latin, Hebrew, Arabic and other scripts.
• Search inside a category, inside a section or everywhere.
• Favorites and Continue watching, with your resume position remembered.
• Hide categories you never want to see.

A PLAYER BUILT FOR STREAMS
• Built-in mpv player with hardware decoding. It plays common formats (HLS, MPEG-TS, MP4, MKV) and reconnects when a stream drops.
• Subtitles from the stream or from a file, and audio track selection.
• Playback speed from 0.25x to 4x for movies and episodes.
• Skip buttons, a sleep timer, fit, fill or stretch, and automatic next episode.
• Catch-up TV and the program guide on channels where your provider supports them.

PICTURE IN PICTURE
A floating window stays on top of your other apps and follows you across Spaces, so you can keep watching while you work.

AND MORE
• Several sources in one library: switch between them, or search them all at once.
• Download movies and episodes from your own source to a folder you choose.
• Back up and restore your sources, favorites and history.
• 31 languages, including right-to-left layouts, and light or dark appearance.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your sources, favorites and history stay on your Mac, and the app talks only to the servers you add. Privacy policy: https://goelir.github.io/IPTVMac/privacy/

GOOD TO KNOW
• Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14 or later.
• Use only sources you are authorized to access. What is available depends on your provider and your country.
• Free and open source: https://github.com/Goelir/IPTVMac
• Support: https://goelir.github.io/IPTVMac/support/ (it also has a demo playlist of open-licence films, so you can try the app without a provider).
