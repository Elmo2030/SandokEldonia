================ PART A: REPLY (paste from the next line to "END OF PART A") ================
Hello, and thank you for the review. Our answers, in the order of your list:

1. Screen recording: attached. It was captured on a physical iPhone (iPhone XS Max, iOS 18.7) and starts with launching the app. It shows adding a source from the review playlist, Test connection, Save and sync, the Live, Movies and Series lists, search, playing a live HLS stream and a movie, and Picture in Picture. The app has no account, login, registration, account deletion, user-generated content, in-app purchase or paid feature, so none of those flows exist.

2. Purpose and audience: IPTVMac is a media player for the user's own IPTV sources (an M3U playlist link or an Xtream Codes login supplied by the user's own provider). It solves the problem of watching those streams in one fast, clean app with instant search, favorites, resume position, subtitles, audio-track selection, playback speed and Picture in Picture. Audience: adults who already subscribe to an IPTV or streaming service and want a good player on iPhone and iPad. The app itself contains no channels, movies or subscriptions; it is rated 18+ by override because users can add any source.

3. Setup: no credentials are needed. On first launch tap "Add source...", choose "M3U", Name: Review, Playlist link: https://goelir.github.io/IPTVMac/review/ReviewPlaylist.m3u, tap Next, "Test connection", then "Save and sync". The playlist holds only open-licence films by the Blender Foundation (Creative Commons Attribution). Try the Live tab "Big Buck Bunny (HLS stream)", the Movies tab "Sintel Trailer (2010)", and the search field.

4. External services: none that we operate or depend on. The app connects only to servers the user enters (the playlist URL or the Xtream server, the stream and logo addresses those sources return). For review, the test playlist is hosted on GitHub Pages (goelir.github.io) and the films are on public hosts (download.blender.org, archive.org, test-streams.mux.dev, demo.unified-streaming.com, upload.wikimedia.org). No authentication service, no payment processor, no analytics, no advertising, no AI service, no developer server. Bundled open-source libraries run on the device: libmpv with FFmpeg (playback) and GRDB (local database).

5. Regional differences: none. The app works the same in every region; what can be played depends only on the user's own provider and local law.

6. Regulated industry and third-party material: the app does not include or provide any third-party content and is not a content provider. Each user adds his or her own source and is responsible for using only sources he or she is authorized to access (the welcome screen says so). The app has no code to bypass copy protection and cannot play DRM-protected streams (FairPlay, Widevine, PlayReady). The review playlist uses only open-licence films (Big Buck Bunny, Sintel, Tears of Steel, Elephants Dream, CC BY 3.0) and the screenshots use invented titles; the demo footage is credited in the description. Because we hold no provider account, there are no credentials to share. We are happy to answer any further question.

Support: https://goelir.github.io/IPTVMac/support/   Privacy: https://goelir.github.io/IPTVMac/privacy/
