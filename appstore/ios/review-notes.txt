IPTVMac is a media player for the user's own IPTV sources (M3U playlist links and Xtream Codes logins). It contains and provides NO content, channels or subscriptions, and it has no account or login of its own: nothing to sign in to, so no demo account is needed.

HOW TO TEST (about 2 minutes)
1. On first launch tap "Add source...". Choose the "M3U" segment. Name: Review. Playlist link: https://goelir.github.io/IPTVMac/review/ReviewPlaylist.m3u then tap Next.
2. Tap "Test connection" ("Connected successfully"), then "Save and sync".
The playlist holds only open-licence films by the Blender Foundation (Big Buck Bunny, Sintel, Tears of Steel, Elephants Dream; Creative Commons Attribution) on public servers: 10 entries, 2 under Live, 5 under Movies plus a trailer, 3 under Series.
Try: tab Live > "Big Buck Bunny (HLS stream)". Tab Movies > "Sintel Trailer (2010)": built-in player; double-tap the sides to skip, swipe up/down on the right for volume, on the left for brightness. The Picture in Picture button in the player's top bar floats the video over other apps; audio keeps playing with the screen locked (audio background mode). Search tab/field: instant search.

THINGS YOU MAY ASK ABOUT
- Xtream Codes: the other source type (Server address, Username, Password). It needs a provider account we cannot supply, because the app has no service of its own. The M3U path above uses the same player and library.
- No downloads: the iOS app cannot save or download media.
- DRM: the app has no code to bypass copy protection. DRM-protected streams (FairPlay, Widevine, PlayReady) do not play.
- Network: the app connects only to servers the user enters and to the logo and stream addresses those sources return. No developer server, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no account. Privacy policy (also inside the app: Settings > About > Privacy): https://goelir.github.io/IPTVMac/privacy/
- ATS: Info.plist sets NSAllowsArbitraryLoads because IPTV providers commonly serve plain http://host:port addresses (often an IP address) that the user types in and that we cannot list in advance.
- Local network: the first time the user adds a source on a private address (a box or server at home) iOS asks for Local Network access; the app never scans the network.
- Encryption: only standard TLS (HTTPS) is used, no proprietary encryption (ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO).
- Age rating: the app itself has no mature material; because users can add any source, we set the rating by override to 18+.
- Open source: the app is free software; source code https://github.com/Goelir/IPTVMac (see the acknowledgements in Settings > About for the bundled libraries, libmpv and FFmpeg under the LGPL).
Support: https://goelir.github.io/IPTVMac/support/
